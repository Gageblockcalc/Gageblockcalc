Gage Block Stack with Wear (Protection) Blocks

Protect your precision blocks by wringing a sacrificial wear block on each end of the stack, then solve the remainder in between.

Formula

Inner = Target − 2 × w (w = 0.050" inch, 1 mm metric)
Stack = w + solve(Inner) + w

Worked example

  1. Subtract the wear blocks. 2.3456 − 2 × 0.050 = 2.2456 in left for the inner stack.
  2. Solve the inner stack. 2.2456 → 0.1006 (clears …6) → 0.145 (clears …45) → 2.000.
  3. Wring wear blocks outside. Place one 0.050" wear block at each end, carbide or marked faces out.
0.050 + 0.1006 + 0.145 + 2.000 + 0.050 = 2.3456 in — 5 blocks including 2 wear blocks.

FAQ

What are wear blocks?

Sacrificial end blocks, usually two 0.050 inch or two 1 mm, often carbide. Measuring contact wears them instead of the precision blocks inside the stack.

Do wear blocks change the target length?

No. They are part of the total. The calculator subtracts both wear blocks from the target and solves the remaining length.

When should I skip wear blocks?

For very short targets under twice the wear block size, or when the set has no wear blocks. The calculator only adds them when the target allows.
