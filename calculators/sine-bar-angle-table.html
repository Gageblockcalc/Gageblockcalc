Sine Bar Angle Table: 5 in, 10 in, and 100 mm

The stack height under a sine bar is H = L × sin(angle). L is the distance between the roll centers, not the overall length of the bar. The angle is in degrees. This table uses three common bars and six angles. Inch heights are rounded to 0.0001 in. Millimetre heights are rounded to 0.001 mm. The blocks are the fewest-block stack from the solver, wear blocks off.

For any other length or angle, use the sine bar calculator. It accepts decimal degrees or degrees, minutes, and seconds.

FAQ

Why is the stack not exactly L times sin(angle)?

Gage blocks move in steps. This table rounds inch heights to 0.0001 in, the smallest step of the 81-piece set, and metric heights to 0.001 mm, the same rounding the sine bar page uses. The difference from the exact sine height is at most half a step.

Which set do the links open?

The 5 in and 10 in columns open the 81-piece inch set. The 100 mm column opens the 88-piece metric set. Wear blocks are off. The link uses the rounded height.

What if my bar is not 5 in, 10 in, or 100 mm?

Use the sine bar calculator on this site. It accepts any length and either decimal degrees or degrees, minutes, and seconds, then opens the solver on the rounded height.
